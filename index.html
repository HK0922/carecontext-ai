CARECONTEXT / FIELD NOTES

Built for questions.
Accountable to sources.

01

Choose a case

Three entirely fictional patient records provide a reproducible starting point. Nothing here is connected to a real EHR.

02

Ask & inspect

Local retrieval ranks passages using BM25. Optional model mode selects from those passages. Displayed statements are complete source excerpts.

03

Preserve uncertainty

Different dated values stay visible. No evidence is a gap, not a negative finding. The app does not diagnose, prescribe, or interpret wound changes.

04

Review & export

Add a human note, mark the review status, and download a Markdown brief with citations, dates, and the corpus fingerprint.

What runs where

Python and FastAPI serve this workspace. SQLite saves questions, evidence snapshots, and review notes locally. The frontend uses browser-native JavaScript and CSS.

Local mode stays on your machine. When explicitly selected and configured, model mode sends your question and retrieved fictional passages to the OpenAI API. Never enter real patient information, including in questions or review notes.

This is a portfolio research prototype, not a clinical system. It has no authentication, clinical validation, EHR connection, or compliance certification.
